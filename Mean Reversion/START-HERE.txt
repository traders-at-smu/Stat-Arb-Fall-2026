SPY BASKET TRACKING STUDY — how to open it
==========================================

QUICKEST WAY
------------
1. Unzip this file (double-click it on Mac, or right-click > Extract All on Windows).
2. Open the folder it creates.
3. Double-click  index.html
It opens in your default browser. That's it — no install, no server, no internet.

IMPORTANT: keep the files together
----------------------------------
index.html loads contrib_data.js from the same folder. If you move index.html on its
own, the page opens but every chart and table will be empty. Move the whole folder.

WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING AT
----------------------
SPY's share price rebuilt two ways over 126 trading days (20 Mar - 18 Sep 2026):
from the 500 S&P constituents, and from the eleven Select Sector SPDR ETFs.
Both start at SPY's real 20 March close of $648.57.

   SPY actual          $761.69
   From 500 equities   $761.96   (27 cents off)
   From sector ETFs    $758.77   ($2.92 off)

FOUR TABS ACROSS THE TOP
------------------------
Charts         Each series on its own, then all three together. On the combined
               chart, use the checkboxes to show/hide any series. Hover anywhere
               on any chart for that day's values.

Daily prices   All 126 sessions. The last two columns are clickable:
                 - click a sector-ETF price  -> the 11 sectors behind it, with
                   each one's weight and dollar contribution. Click any sector
                   row again -> every stock inside that sector.
                 - click an equity price     -> top 10 contributors, then
                   "Show all 506" for the full list.
               Contributions always add up to the price shown.

Sector weights 11 sparklines showing each sector's weight moving across the
               window, the index-weight vs ETF-AUM-weight comparison, and the
               full daily weights table.

Why the gap    Why the sector ETFs don't reproduce SPY: the Select Sector 24/50
               capping rule. Includes each ETF measured against the stocks it
               actually holds, plus the data notes.

BROWSER NOTES
-------------
Works in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge. Nothing loads from the internet except
the web fonts, so it renders fine offline (fonts just fall back to system ones).
Dark mode follows your OS setting automatically.
